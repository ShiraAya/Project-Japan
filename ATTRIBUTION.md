# Data and reference attribution

## GSHHG / GSHHS coastline

The bundled `japan_land_hd.png` coastline mask is derived from the Global Self-consistent, Hierarchical, High-resolution Geography Database (GSHHG, formerly GSHHS), accessed through the Basemap intermediate-resolution data package. GSHHG is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License. A copy of LGPL-3.0 is included at `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/GSHHG-LGPL-3.txt`.

The former Project Japan Japan-only mask is used only to select Japanese GSHHG land components and exclude mainland Asia.

## Elevation

The bundled `japan_height.png` is a resampled derivative of DEM10B PNG elevation tiles
provided by the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI / 国土地理院):

- Tile list and source attribution: https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html
- Elevation PNG encoding specification: https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/demtile.html

The z=9 tiles were decoded according to the official signed-centimetre RGB specification,
reprojected to the Project Japan bounds (122..154 E, 20..46 N), bilinearly resampled to
`8192 x 6656`, rounded to integer metres, and masked to the bundled Japanese land coverage.
The source metadata is bundled beside the raster as `japan_height_metadata.json`.

This game-oriented derivative must not be used for surveying or navigation. Retain the GSI
attribution and follow the applicable GSI content-use rules when redistributing it.


## Hydrology references

River centre-lines, names, direction/connectivity and endpoint elevation were checked against
the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) National Land Numerical
Information W05 River specification and official basin references where route corrections were
needed. Lake and lagoon controls were checked against MLIT W09 Lake polygons/attributes and GSI
lake-survey references for area, water level and maximum depth.

Official reference pages:

- https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-W05.html
- https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-W09-v2_2.html
- https://www.gsi.go.jp/kankyochiri/koshouchousa-list.html
- https://www.gsi.go.jp/kankyochiri/lakedata.html

This alpha does not redistribute the original W05/W09 or GSI lake-survey archives. Version 1.0.0
adds compressed derivative coverage catalogs generated by `tools/hydrology/`: W05 stream topology
and metadata are consolidated into logical entities, and W09 polygons are simplified at a 24 m
tolerance for PJ's 1:8 scale. The currently generated 115 river centre-lines and 33 lake control
lobes remain manually authored gameplay geometry and override overlapping official records.

The W05 source page marks the river data for non-commercial use; W09 is marked commercial-use
permitted. Anyone redistributing or using the derivative catalogs must review and follow the
current MLIT terms linked above. ProjectJapan's data and geometry are game-oriented derivatives,
not survey products, and are unsuitable for navigation, flood analysis or engineering work.
