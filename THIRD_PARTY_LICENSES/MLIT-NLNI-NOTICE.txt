The bundled Project Japan resources are edited/processed game derivatives. They directly project
JGD2000 geometry, round and simplify coordinates, derive game widths and vertical profiles, and
encode the result in a custom index. They are not official MLIT products.

The W05 data page marks the river dataset "non-commercial". The provider's applicable terms,
attribution requirements, limitations and downstream notice requirements remain applicable.
Review the current linked terms before use or redistribution.
