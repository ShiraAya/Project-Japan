ProjectJapanRefined contains processed geographic derivatives, not official MLIT
products. Its independently written compiler projects and simplifies source
geometry, selects river routes, and preserves lake rings. Inferred connectors are
identified in the accompanying audit. Game water levels, widths, banks and beds
are computed by the PJR implementation and are not surveyed MLIT attributes.
Exact original archive URLs and SHA-256 values appear in data/source-lock.json.

The W05 data page marks the river dataset "non-commercial". The provider's
applicable data terms and attribution requirements remain attached to the data.
These notices do not change the licensing of the separately written PJR code.
